Mine Knowledge. Not Hashes.

Last updated: 09 Sep 2026 · Download Miner · Questions? See FAQ

Why not an Antminer?

An Antminer S19 Pro burns 3,250W at 75dB and lost about $3.47/day in August 2026 — it heats your room and empties your wallet. A NEMES miner uses 60–240W silently and turns the same socket into verified knowledge vectors that accumulate shares. Electricity becomes knowledge, not heat.

Pick your tier

Tier 1 — Normal PC (CPU)
Parsing + light embedding + audit votes. ~65–100W. Multiplier K=0.5. No model download needed.
Tier 2 — Gamer GPU 7B (8–12GB VRAM)
Embedding + 7B chat node. Models: Qwen2.5-7B, Llama-3.1-8B, Gemma-2-9B (Q4_K_M GGUF from HuggingFace). ~170–200W. Multiplier K=1.0.
Tier 3 — Workstation 30B (24GB VRAM)
Embedding + 30B RAG node. Models: Qwen2.5-32B GGUF. ~350–450W. Multiplier K=2.0.
Tier 4 — DGX Spark 70B (128GB unified, 140–240W)
70B fine-tuning + inference up to 200B parameters. Models: Llama-3.1-70B, Qwen2.5-72B. Cheaper than a new Antminer, 1/13th the power. Multiplier K=5.0.
Tier 5 — Server (multi-GPU, pro)
Flagship open models: DeepSeek-V3 (671B MoE) and Kimi-K2 (1T MoE) via vLLM/SGLang on H100-class hardware. Teacher nodes for distillation. Multiplier K=10. Practical shortcut: R1-Distill-32B/70B on Tier 3–4.

Earnings

Shares = verified vectors × tier multiplier. 20 proofs = 1 batch (0.002 NEMES base, halving every 5M batches). Pool = 50% of network revenue, split by shares. No minimum, dust accumulates. Phase 1: shares accumulate on ledger. Phase 2: cash payout from treasury + enterprise revenue. Nothing here is financial advice.

Plain-language economics

Total supply is fixed at 210M coins — no more can ever be printed. Your computer earns shares for verified knowledge (stronger machine = more shares). Every 20 proofs make one box, with rewards halving roughly every 3 years — early miners earn more. Companies pay subscriptions; half goes to miners weekly, split by shares. Example: $1,000 pool, your 1,000 of 100,000 shares = $10. Honest note: the coin has no exchange price today. Nobody promises riches — your electricity is 1/13th of an Antminer and your shares accumulate as the network grows.

How rewards work, step by step

1. You work: each accepted proof earns shares (tier multiplier applies). 2. Box closes: 20 proofs = 1 box with a fixed reward. 3. Audit: 1% of proofs are re-checked — failures are deleted, cheaters earn strikes. 4. Week closes: half of company revenue plus treasury top-up forms the pool, split by shares (minus 2% fee, $5 minimum). 5. Era turns roughly every 3 years: box rewards halve, but a growing network means a growing pool. 6. Long term: box rewards stop at 0.5 and never hit zero; if emission ever ends, the revenue pool pays alone — like fees taking over in Bitcoin.

Setup in 5 minutes

1. Download the signed client above and verify its SHA256.

2. Create a key (nemes-miner keygen), optionally pledge storage (nemes-miner keygen --storage). No root needed.

3. Run: nemes-miner mine --simple --komuta https://komuta.nemes-x.space --corpus tr. Ctrl+C stops gracefully — your shares stay on the ledger.

Your data stays yours

Miners see only blind micro-batches (salted IDs, no corpus names). Canary trap texts catch leakers: proven leaks mean ban + forfeited shares. Unplugging is always free — only proven cheating is destroyed. Full spec: Security Architecture.
