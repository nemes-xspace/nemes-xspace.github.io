DMCA Policy

Last updated: 30 Aug 2026

1. Copyright

NEMES-X processes publicly available corpora (e.g., Wikipedia, arXiv, Common Crawl derivatives). We respect the intellectual property rights of creators and comply with applicable copyright laws. The knowledge index stores mathematical vector embeddings and short search snippets — not full original documents.

2. Reporting Infringement

If you believe that material available through the NEMES-X network or its hosted search features infringes your copyright, please send a written notice to nemes-x.space@zohomail.eu (subject: [DMCA]) (once email is live) or via the official GitHub repository with the following information:

  • A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or their authorized agent.
  • Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
  • Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, with sufficient detail to locate it (e.g., corpus name, document ID, search snippet).
  • Your contact information (name, address, email, phone).
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf.

3. Action We Will Take

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will promptly investigate and, where appropriate:

  • Remove or disable access to the identified vector snippets or search results from the hosted index.
  • Notify the affected miner nodes to exclude the disputed content from future premium/hosted services.
  • Add the content to a public blocklist to prevent re-embedding.

4. Counter-Notification

If you believe material was removed in error, you may file a counter-notification with the same contact channel, including:

  • Your contact information and signature.
  • Identification of the removed material and its prior location.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good faith belief the material was removed due to mistake or misidentification.
  • A statement consenting to the jurisdiction of your local district court.

5. Repeat Infringers

We reserve the right to terminate or restrict premium/hosted access for users or miner nodes that repeatedly submit infringing content.
